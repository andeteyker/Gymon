# GYMON Product Spec v0.2

## Core loop
Real training → personal progress → muscle/performance stats → Gymon XP → moves/evolution → battles → motivation to train again.

## Product principle
The free product must remain a genuinely useful training app. Core training quality, progressive overload and dynamic workout adaptation are not paywalled. Gymon+ monetizes automation, deeper intelligence, convenience and personalization — never combat power.

## MVP / vertical slice
1. Onboarding: goal, experience, training days and starter selection.
2. Select Voltex as the first fully implemented starter.
3. View or start a workout.
4. Complete/edit prescribed sets, reps and load with minimal input.
5. Persist workout history.
6. Dynamically adapt future prescriptions using progressive overload.
7. Convert productive personal progress into normalized Gymon progression.
8. Gain XP, level up and evolve.
9. Unlock/equip a move.
10. Complete one deterministic PvE battle.

## Free
- Workout logging and history
- Progressive overload
- Dynamic plan/workout adaptation
- Gymon XP, levels, evolutions and collection
- Core PvE game loop
- Basic statistics
- Manual/imported training plans
- Plausibility-aware progression scoring

## Gymon+
Gymon+ is the premium layer. Its flagship feature is automatic training-plan creation based on goals, experience, available training days, equipment and preferred session duration.

Planned Gymon+ value:
- Automatic training-plan creation
- Deeper training analytics
- Advanced AI/coach functionality later
- Additional convenience and personalization
- Additional Gymon/team slots where this does not create combat power
- Selected premium cosmetic rewards

Dynamic plan adaptation and progression remain free.

## Monetization guardrails
- No purchasable combat power.
- No paid XP multipliers.
- No faster physical progression for paying users.
- Cosmetics may be sold separately.
- Seasonal cosmetic/reward passes may be added later.
- Gym/brand partnerships are a later B2B opportunity.

## Anti-cheat / progression integrity
Gymon XP must never scale directly with absolute lifted weight. Progression is based primarily on productive workout completion, personal improvement, consistency and goal adherence.

Workout data receives a trust/plausibility assessment. Implausible entries may remain in the user's training log while their Gymon XP contribution is capped or excluded from competitive systems.

Potential signals include:
- implausible load/repetition combinations
- extreme jumps versus personal history
- impossible or highly improbable workout timing
- bulk backdated sessions
- abnormal volume spikes

Strict verification is primarily required for leaderboards, competitions, rare competitive rewards or real-world prizes. Normal single-player progression should avoid intrusive anti-cheat.

## Confirmed game decisions
- Fitness tracker first, game layer second.
- Three bipedal starter families with clearly different silhouettes/evolution trees.
- Voltex and Nimba are current concept starters; third starter remains open.
- No elemental type system.
- Seven evolution stages per long-term path.
- 100+ forms are a long-term target with secret branches.
- Evolution choice is gated by level plus training-derived requirements.
- De-evolution is allowed outside battle. Sacrificed progress can grant capped meta tokens, never direct paid combat strength.
- Four equipped moves at a time; learned moves remain available.
- Moves unlock through muscle/performance levels and certain evolutions.
- Turn-based battles; priority then speed determines order.
- PvE first; PvP/guilds later.
- Rest/overtraining is informative, not punitive.
- Excess volume has diminishing/capped rewards.
- Nutrition is outside v0.1.

## Vertical slice acceptance
A new user can complete onboarding, select Voltex, complete a workout, receive plausible personal progression, see the next workout adapt, level Voltex, unlock/equip a move and finish one deterministic PvE battle.
